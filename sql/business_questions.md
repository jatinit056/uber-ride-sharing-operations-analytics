# SQL business questions

Every query in `sql/analysis_queries.sql` answers one business question. Results are in `sql/results/` (generated by `scripts/run_sql_queries.py`); the answers below are taken from those files.

| # | Business question | SQL technique | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q01 | How many trips are in each period, and over how many days? | GROUP BY, COUNT DISTINCT, UNION ALL view | 18,804,806 trips: 4,534,327 in 183 days (2014) and 14,270,479 in 181 days (2015) |
| Q02 | How did monthly trips grow month over month? | CTE, LAG window, WINDOW clause | Largest 2015 rise in trips per day: 2015-02 (+28.3%); largest 2014 rise: 2014-09 (+28.1%) |
| Q03 | How much did demand grow year over year (April-June 2014 vs 2015)? | CTE, self-JOIN, UNION ALL | Apr-Jun 2015 = 4.14× Apr-Jun 2014 (+314.4%) |
| Q04 | Which weekdays are busiest in 2015? | AVG, RANK window | Busiest: Saturday (92,868/day); quietest: Monday (65,164/day) |
| Q05 | Which hours are the peak and the trough of the day (2015)? | CTE, MAX/MIN OVER (), CASE | Peak 19:00, trough 4:00, ratio 5.79× |
| Q06 | What does the weekday x hour demand matrix look like (average trips, 2015)? | AVG … FILTER (pivot) | 24 × 7 matrix of average trips (heat map in the notebook and Excel) |
| Q07 | What is the peak hour of each weekday (2015)? | CTE, ROW_NUMBER PARTITION BY | Mon 18:00; Tue 18:00; Wed 19:00; Thu 19:00; Fri 19:00; Sat 23:00; Sun 0:00 |
| Q08 | Which days of 2015 were the busiest and the quietest? | RANK, LEFT JOIN holidays | Busiest 2015-06-27 (136,193); quietest 2015-01-27 (25,954) |
